NVIDIA Blackwell B200 source dossier

This is a source dossier for the NVIDIA Blackwell B200 data center GPU. It brings together NVIDIA documentation, research papers, independent measurements, and benchmark results, with a short guide to what each source covers and where to look.

The four main topics are: microarchitecture (SM organization, register file, functional units, fifth-generation tensor cores and Tensor Memory, scheduling); the ISA and memory consistency model (PTX, tcgen05, TMA, scopes and proxies); the memory system and interconnect (dual-die design, caches, HBM3e, NVLink); and specialized units and performance (Decompression Engine, Transformer Engine, MLPerf).

Prepared by Luke Abram, Pranav Subramanian, Aidan Szilagyi, and Abhi Satpathy · University of Virginia.
