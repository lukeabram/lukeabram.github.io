Microbenchmarking NVIDIA’s Blackwell Architecture: An in-depth Architectural Analysis

The main academic source for this report, because it measures an actual B200 rather than a consumer part. It uses PTX microbenchmarks on TMEM, the Decompression Engine, the tensor cores, and FP4/FP6, and compares the results against H200.

Read on arXiv.

First submitted 1 December 2025; section numbers refer to v3 (March 2026). Used in sections A, C, D.

Where to look

  • Blackwell Architecture, §III-A. 148 SMs over eight GPCs, four L2 partitions, eight HBM3e stacks, and NV-HBI.
  • Tensor Memory, §V-A. TMEM as 256 KB per SM, organized as 512 columns × 128 lanes of 32-bit cells.
  • Decompression Engine, §V-B. Measured output throughput of roughly 160–220 GB/s.
  • 5th-Generation Tensor Cores, §VI-A, and FP4/FP6, §VI-B. Measured dense throughput by precision.
  • Mixed-Precision Training, §VII-D. Training speedup over H200 attributed to SM count (1.09×), CTA pairing (1.27×), and TMEM (1.26×).
  • FP64, §VII-C1. Useful for the question of where B200 does not improve.
