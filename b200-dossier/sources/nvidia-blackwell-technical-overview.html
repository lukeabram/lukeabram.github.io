NVIDIA Blackwell Architecture Technical Overview

NVIDIA’s own description of the Blackwell platform. It is the vendor primary source for the dual-die design, SM diagrams and unit counts, and the specialized engines. Treat performance claims here as marketing until a measurement source confirms them.

Read at NVIDIA.

Access: NVIDIA’s resource library may ask for contact details before the PDF opens.

Section names below follow the whitepaper’s topic headings; check them against the copy you download. Used in sections A, C, D.

Where to look

  • Dual-die design and NV-HBI. How two reticle-limited dies are joined and presented to software as one GPU (Sections A and C).
  • Second-generation Transformer Engine. Micro-tensor scaling and FP4/FP6 support (Sections A and D).
  • Decompression Engine, RAS Engine, Confidential Computing. The specialized units covered in Section D.
  • Fifth-generation NVLink and NVLink Switch. Per-GPU link bandwidth and fabric size (Section C).
